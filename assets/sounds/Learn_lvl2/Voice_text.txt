# Level 2 topic 1 (intro)
========================================================================================

Welcome back!
In the previous level, we learned the names of the Arabic alphabet, such as ألف، باء، تاء، ثاء.

Learning the alphabet names first is very important, because it will help us learn the different sounds of the Arabic alphabet.

Today, we're going to learn about the Arabic alphabet sounds.
When we read Arabic words like this one, we don’t use the letters’ names, like راء، زاي، قاف, to pronounce the word — instead, we use the sounds of the letters.

Each letter will have a different sound when we add a diacritic to it.

Diacritics are small marks that are placed on top of or underneath a letter, and they change the letter’s sound and help us read the word correctly.

So get ready… and let’s start learning the different sounds for each letter using diacritics!

========================================================================================
# Level 2 topic 2 (Fat-Ha)
========================================================================================
The first diacritic we are going to learn is called فَتحَه. فَتحَه is a small diagonal line that is placed on top of a letter. 
It adds a sound like "AA" to the end of the letter's pronunciation. 
For example, if we add فَتحَه to the letter تاء, it's pronounced تا.
Similarly if we add فَتحَه to the letter قاف, it's pronounced قا.
Now, let's try adding فَتحَه to the Arabic alphabet one by one!


ألف with فَتحَه becomes ءا.
باء with فَتحَه becomes با.
تاء with فَتحَه becomes تا.
ثاء with فَتحَه becomes ثا.

خاء with فَتحَه becomes خا.
دال with فَتحَه becomes دا.
ذال with فَتحَه becomes ذا.
راء with فَتحَه becomes را.
زاي with فَتحَه becomes زا.
سين with فَتحَه becomes سا.
شين with فَتحَه becomes شا.
صاد with فَتحَه becomes صا.
ضاد with فَتحَه becomes ضا.
طاء with فَتحَه becomes طا.
ظاء with فَتحَه becomes ظا.
عين with فَتحَه becomes عا.
غين with فَتحَه becomes غا.
فاء with فَتحَه becomes فا.
قاف with فَتحَه becomes قا.
كاف with فَتحَه becomes كا.
لام with فَتحَه becomes لا.

# Level 2 topic 3 (KAS-RAH)
========================================================================================
The second diacritic we are going to learn is called كَسرَه.
كَسْرَه is a small diagonal line that looks very similar to فَتْحه!

So what is the difference?

Well, my friend, there are two big differences:

First, كَسرَه is placed "Under" the letter.

Second, It adds a sound like “EE” to the letter’s pronunciation.

For example, if we add كَسرَه to the letter نون, it’s pronounced نِي.
Similarly, if we add كَسرَه to the letter كاف, it’s pronounced كِي.

Now, let’s try adding كَسرَه to the Arabic alphabet one by one!

ألف with كَسرَه becomes اِ.
باء with كَسرَه becomes بي.
تاء with كَسرَه becomes تي.

# Level 2 topic 4 (DAM-MAH)
========================================================================================
In this lesson, we are going to learn about the ضَمه diacritic.

ضَمه looks similar to a small وُاو that is placed on top of a letter!

It adds a sound like “oo”, to the letter’s pronunciation.

For example, if we add ضَمه to the letter ثاء, it's pronounced ثُو.

Similarly, if we add ضَمه to the letter جيم, it's pronounced جُو.

Now, let's try adding ضَمه to the Arabic alphabet one by one!
